Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about building an accessory dwelling unit in Orange County, California.

How much does it cost to build an ADU in Orange County?
Turnkey ADU construction in Orange County typically ranges from $149,000 for a compact 350 sq ft studio/JADU to $245,000 for a spacious 2-bedroom home. Garage conversions start from $95,000. All ADU Alliance contracts include architectural drafting, structural engineering, 100% city permitting, and full ground-up construction.
How long does it take to obtain ADU building permits in Orange County?
Under California state law (Gov. Code § 65852.2), local agencies must act on ADU permit applications within 60 days. In Orange County, the full process from initial drafting to permit in hand averages 60 to 90 days.
Can my HOA stop me from building an ADU?
No. Under California Assembly Bill 670, Homeowners Associations (HOAs) are legally prohibited from banning or unreasonably restricting ADU construction. We manage all HOA design review submissions as part of our turnkey service.
Can I sell my ADU separately as a condominium?
Yes. California Assembly Bill 1033 allows local municipalities to adopt ordinances permitting ADUs to be sold separately as condominiums, unlocking major equity value for homeowners. We advise clients on the specific condominium conversion requirements in their city.
Do you offer financing for ADU projects?
Yes. We partner with premier residential lenders offering Home Equity Lines of Credit (HELOCs), construction-to-permanent loans, and cash-out refinancing with $0 down options to fund your build.
