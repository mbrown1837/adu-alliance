Forms post directly to `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/info@adualliance.com` with agency notification CC to `james.delacey01@gmail.com`.

### First-Time Activation Step:
1. Submit a test lead on the live site.
2. FormSubmit will send a confirmation link to `info@adualliance.com`.
3. Click the **"Activate Form"** link in that email.
4. From that point on, all lead submissions are delivered instantly to your inbox and logged to GA4!
